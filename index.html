Keyboard shortcuts
  • Space or K: play / pause
  • Left / Right: back / forward 5 seconds
  • J / L: back / forward 10 seconds
  • Up / Down: volume up / down
  • M: mute, F: full screen, R: loop on / off, N: next in queue
  • 0 - 9: jump to 0% - 90% of the video
  • C: subtitles on / off
  • ?: show or hide this list
  • A / B: set loop points A and B, X: clear the A-B loop
  • T: cinema mode on / off
  • S: save this moment
  • , / .: previous / next saved moment

          Help and FAQ

          How do I play a video?

          Paste any YouTube link in the first box and tap Load video. You can also add several links to the queue and they will play one after another.

          Can I download YouTube videos?

          No. YouTube does not allow downloading, so this app plays YouTube links in the player instead. The downloader works for many other sites, for example TikTok, TED, SoundCloud and Streamable.

          Why does the downloader say "waking up the free server"?

          The download helper runs on a free server that sleeps when nobody uses it. The first request wakes it, which can take up to a minute. After that it is fast.

          How do I add a whole YouTube playlist?

          Paste the playlist link in the first box and tap "Add playlist to queue". Every video in the list is added to your queue (up to the 50-video queue limit).

          Why is YouTube volume limited to 100%?

          YouTube's own player does not allow more than 100%. For louder sound, open a file from your device in the "Play a file" card - it can boost volume up to 300%.

          Where are my queue and favourites saved?

          In this browser, on this device only. Use "Export backup" in Your videos to save them, with your settings, to a file, and "Import backup" to load everything on another device or browser.

          Can I listen with the phone screen off?

          See the Listening help card below the player for simple steps and the screen-off limits.

          The page is very long. Can I hide parts of it?

          Yes. Tap a section's title or the round arrow next to it to fold that section away, and tap again to bring it back. Your choice is remembered on this device. The "Collapse all" button at the top folds everything in one tap.

          Does the app work offline?

          Once installed, the app opens without internet and your queue, favourites and settings are still there. Playing videos and downloading need internet.

          What is sent anywhere?

          Your queue, favourites and settings never leave your device. The download server only receives the link you ask it to fetch, and its files are deleted after 15 minutes. This app shows no ads of its own and has no trackers.
